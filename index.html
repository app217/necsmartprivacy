Effective Date: November 8, 2026

Welcome to NEC Smartpay. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the NEC Smartpay mobile application and related services.

Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, and other details you provide.

  • Transaction Information: Transaction IDs, amounts, dates, payment status, and relevant recipient or payment details.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, app version, and technical diagnostic information where collected.
  • Support Information: Information you provide when contacting customer support.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate NEC Smartpay services.
  • Process and manage transactions.
  • Verify user information where required.
  • Maintain account security and prevent unauthorized activity.
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • Improve application performance and reliability.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary with:

  • Payment processors, banks, or relevant financial service providers.
  • Technology, hosting, and infrastructure service providers.
  • Security, fraud prevention, or technical support providers.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. Data transmission should use appropriate security protections, such as HTTPS, where supported.

However, no electronic storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain transaction records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, and protect our services. When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymize it, where applicable.

6. Account and Data Deletion

If NEC Smartpay provides account registration, users may request account deletion through the available in-app option or by contacting our support team.

After receiving a valid request, we will process deletion of the account and associated personal data, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, security, and transaction record-retention requirements.

NEC Smartpay is not intended for children who are below the minimum age permitted to use the relevant services. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law.
